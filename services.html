Home/Services

Start with the situation, not a preset package. These services can be discussed individually or as part of a broader security plan for people, property or operations.

01 / PEOPLE

Security guarding

On-site security presence, with duties and procedures considered for the premises and its day-to-day activity.

02 / PERSONAL

V.I.P. protection

Personal protection planning for individuals, with discretion and movement requirements in mind.

03 / DIGITAL

Cyber security

Support to identify digital-security concerns and consider practical steps to strengthen resilience.

04 / PREMISES

Access control

Approaches to help manage who can enter a site, which areas they can reach and how access is recorded.

05 / MONITORING

CCTV surveillance

Surveillance planning and monitoring options aligned to visibility, operations and privacy considerations.

06 / CANINE

Security dogs

Canine security support for appropriate environments, delivered with attention to safe handling and welfare.

07 / REVIEW

Forensic audit

A structured review of available records or processes to help identify anomalies and support informed follow-up.

08 / READINESS

Risk assessment

Review potential threats, vulnerabilities and impacts to help prioritise proportionate security measures.

09 / AUTHORISED TESTING

Ethical hacking

Security testing only with explicit authorisation and an agreed scope, aimed at finding weaknesses responsibly.

Specific service availability, scope, authorisations and operating arrangements should be confirmed directly with Brillex before engagement.
